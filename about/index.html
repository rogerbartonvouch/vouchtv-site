VouchTV

A film and TV discovery app for iPhone, in private beta.

Every pick on VouchTV is a real, named person’s actually-stated recommendation — filmmakers, critics, actors, musicians — with a citation to where they said it. There are no star ratings and no aggregate scores.

It is built for the moment you sit down to watch something: a few good answers, from people you trust, on the streaming services you already have.

Your lists are private by default. There is no feed and no follower count.

VouchTV was founded by film editor Roger Barton.
